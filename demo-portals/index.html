Optional evidence library

A place for the rest. When it’s useful.

Payslips, property, investments and household records have distinct meanings. These downloadable examples establish those boundaries; they do not turn on app features.

Experimental · evidence only. These are synthetic CSV schemas for design and onboarding exploration. They are not wired into the core importer, certified provider formats, or tax calculations.

What this proves: three fixture formats can be normalised, reviewed, exported and read back locally. Duplicate files add no rows; real repeated charges survive.

What it does not prove: Google Sheets/Drive delivery, real bank export compatibility, completed tax readiness, or any optional-domain ingestion. No real credentials should ever be entered here.

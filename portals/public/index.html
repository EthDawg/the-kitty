Every portal, one household

Where the Davies' money files come from.

The Kitty reads one Google Sheet. The sheet is fed by files a household downloads, or finds in its inbox, from everyone who sends it paper. These parody portals hold the fictional Davies household's whole financial life, from the day the home settled in March 2019. Seven generate exports in a real institution's exact shape; every portal also hands over the files as they were delivered: PDFs, CSVs, JSON, emails, saved pages, spreadsheets and scans. Sign in, take a year or a month, run the importer, and an agent can build the sheet from nothing but these files.

The rules

  • No real marks. Every name on this page is a parody with an original wordmark. The column shapes are the real ones; the names and logos are not.
  • No real credentials, ever. Any name signs in with the password demo. The bank-export skill never types, reads or stores a password or a one-time code; the person or their password manager does that, here as anywhere.
  • The files agree with the sheet. The six years before the sheet starts land, to the cent, on the sheet's opening balances; every closed month downloaded here is byte-identical to the file the month-end simulator put under sources/; the two months ahead come from the same simulator. Loan, owner, payslip, income-statement and renewal documents are derived from those rows, never invented beside them.
  • Files as delivered stay as delivered. Under "Files as delivered", each download is the file the Davies world wrote, byte for byte. Councils, water and energy share one portal each to keep this page short; the stream names who sent what.
  • Sign in as someone. One click on "Continue as Daisy" or "Continue as Danny", no password, shows only what that person holds, with the limits a real site has. For now they stand in for Jordan and Mia, the people in these files. Any name with the password demo shows the whole household.
  • There is a clock. Add ?chapter=1 or ?today=2027-01-31 to a portal's address, and nothing later exists. How the sites work, for an agent or a person.
  • One account is not on the sheet. Jordan's old everyday at CommBark exists only here, so the CommBank shape has a real account behind it. It is labelled.

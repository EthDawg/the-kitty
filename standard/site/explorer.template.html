Changing a rate must not change the past.

A loan has no interest rate. It has a history of rates, and a history of when each was known. Every view answers two dates: the day the question is about, and the day it is asked. In 2025 the lender cut the rate, then backdated the cut by a week. Move the dates.

Accuracy is what cannot be false at once.

Numbers in a table are claims. These are the laws that check them. A gap is missing evidence. A fail is evidence contradicting evidence. No law catches a wrong category, because a wrong category conserves perfectly. The eval's truth catches that here; on a real household only review does.

Containers are code. Institutions are data.

No per-bank adapters. A recipe is a small piece of data a cheap model can write from a few rows, cached by the shape of the file, so the second household pays nothing. The gate does not care who wrote it: money is conserved, and misreading it breaks conservation.

# The Kitty for iPhone and iPad

**To get help, [open a support issue](https://github.com/EthDawg/the-kitty/issues/new).** The issue is public. Describe which screen you used, what you expected and what appeared. Use the fictional household to reproduce a problem where possible. Keep financial records, bank files, account numbers, private Sheet links and sign-in details out of reports and screenshots.

**Getting started.** Open Home to explore the fictional Davies household without signing in. Activity shows the entries behind the totals. In Ask the Kitty, try a question about spending, then tap a source entry to inspect the figures. Setup shows whether you are exploring the offline example or viewing a live Sheet.

**Opening a live Sheet.** In Setup, select the Google Sheet connection and follow Google's picker. Open the selected Sheet to load its figures. Google controls which spreadsheets you can see and whether you may edit them. Use the fictional household for practice and keep personal records out of its shared demo Sheet.

**Checking a category save.** If a save was interrupted, reopen the same Google account and Sheet. Use **Check this save** if it appears in Setup. Kitty can then read the decision back before you try another edit.

**Finding an export.** Activity exports the filtered entries. Look in the folder or provider you selected in Files. Cancelling the picker leaves the export unsaved.

**Disconnecting.** Select **Disconnect Google** in Setup to stop using that connection. If Google cannot confirm revocation, [manage the connection in your Google account](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/13533235). Changes already saved to the Sheet remain available in Google Sheets. You can remove exported files from their destination in Files.
